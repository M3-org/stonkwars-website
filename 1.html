About Stonk Wars

Stonk Wars is a 24/7 interactive AI-powered livestream where virtual characters inhabit a crypto-themed NYSE trading floor. It's experimental software exploring new territory at the intersection of AI, entertainment, and decentralized communities.

What starts as derpy market commentary evolves into something bigger. AI agents don't just react to price charts—they read chat, develop personalities, and create moments that resonate across crypto Twitter. We're building entertainment that responds, not just broadcasts.

But here's where it gets interesting: this framework could power collaborative governance for DAOs. Imagine AI agents facilitating community decisions through entertaining debates, or characters representing different factions (DeFi maximalists vs. memecoin degens) competing for influence. Token dynamics could determine character power, survival, even storylines.

We're starting with market analysis because it's fun, fast-moving, and crypto-native. But the vision extends to collaborative agentic governance—blending entertainment with politics, where communities don't just vote on proposals, they watch their values represented through AI characters in real-time narratives.

Think: "The West Wing" meets crypto governance, 24/7, shaped by your participation.

  • → Governance Infrastructure: Framework for DAOs to turn decision-making into engaging narratives
